## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an executive, prestigious, and rigorous digital environment for Stipk Palacio Díaz, Public Accountant specialized in fiscal auditing (*revisoría fiscal*) and forensic audit in Cartagena, Colombia. 

The aesthetic is grounded in **Corporate Modernism** infused with **Material Design 3** systematic discipline, tailored specifically to financial governance and high-stakes auditing. It departs drastically from cold bureaucratic accounting tools, generic corporate law firms, or casual startup SaaS aesthetics.

### Brand Attributes & Voice
- **Authoritative & Exact:** Grounded in fiscal precision, forensic scrutiny, and 10 years of validated corporate experience.
- **Sober & Prestigious:** Built on deep naval depths with measured, selective warm gold accents that evoke stability and institutional trust.
- **Conversion-Driven:** Relentlessly focused on two clear transactional paths without friction: booking a free inaugural advisory session or initiating immediate dialogue via WhatsApp.

### Visual Architecture
The UI establishes calm reassurance through generous negative space, crisp geometric typography, subtle ambient elevation, and deliberate information density that guides decision-makers (CEOs, CFOs, business owners) from initial assessment through consultation.

## Colors

The color system operates primarily on an authoritative deep-navy canvas (`#06182B` and `#071A2E`), accented strategically with refined gold (`#E4B25F`).

### Role-Based Applications
- **Dominant Canvas (`#06182B`, `#071A2E`):** Establishes the institutional, high-trust tone across hero surfaces, structural navigation, and highlighted service panels.
- **Primary Accent (`#E4B25F`):** Reserved exclusively for focal points, high-priority conversion targets ("Agendar primera asesoría gratis"), key metrics, and precision linework. Never applied to decorative large fills.
- **Secondary Accent (`#ECBD6A`):** Designated for interactive focus states, hovers on primary CTAs, and active icon accents.
- **Secondary Surfaces & Cards (`#FFFFFF`, `#F5F0E6`):** Used selectively in light-themed content modules (e.g., standard service inspection cards) to balance visual density and deliver crisp typographic legibility.

### Accessibility & Contrast Compliance (WCAG AA)
- Gold `#E4B25F` paired with Navy `#06182B` achieves a contrast ratio of > 8.5:1, exceeding WCAG AAA for both standard and large text.
- White `#FFFFFF` on Navy `#06182B` achieves > 16:1 contrast ratio.
- Secondary elements, subtle borders, and placeholder treatments must never fall below the 4.5:1 threshold against their respective background containers.

## Typography

The type scale uses **Montserrat** systematically across all roles to project architectural clarity, balance, and modern corporate strength.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Role Directives
- **Headlines (Weights 600 & 700):** Tight line heights (1.1–1.2) with restrained negative letter spacing create solid editorial impact in hero sections and conversion headers.
- **Body Text (Weight 400):** Relaxed line heights (1.55–1.6) provide effortless legibility for complex regulatory and advisory descriptions. Body text never drops below 16px on mobile viewports.
- **Data & Navigation (Weight 500):** Applied across breadcrumbs, meta labels, and statistical callouts to establish secondary hierarchy.
- **Interactive Triggers & Buttons (Weight 600):** Subtle letter spacing (`0.02em`) preserves distinction and readability under pressure.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is constructed on a strict **4px vertical and horizontal grid system** that guarantees structural precision across all device widths.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** Max container width `1200px`. 12-column symmetrical grid with `24px` (`1.5rem`) gutters and horizontal margins of `32px` to `48px`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with `20px` to `24px` gutters and `24px` margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid with `16px` (`1rem`) gutters and fixed `20px` (`1.25rem`) side padding.

### Rhythm & Vertical Flow
- **Macro Spacing:** Section padding spans `80px` to `128px` on desktop and `56px` to `80px` on mobile, maintaining an unhurried, executive pacing.
- **Micro Spacing:** Element stacks within cards and form fields use strictly derived multiples of 4px (`4px`, `8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `40px`).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses a **tactful tonal and ambient elevation model** adapted from Material Design 3, avoiding heavy skeuomorphic shadows or aggressive drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
1. **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** `#06182B` (deep dark) or `#F5F0E6` (editorial cream). No shadow.
2. **Level 1 (Subtle Containers & Regular Cards):** `#071A2E` surface or `#FFFFFF` card with a 1px hairline border (`rgba(228, 178, 95, 0.15)` on navy, or `rgba(6, 24, 43, 0.08)` on light) plus ambient diffusion: `0 2px 8px rgba(6, 24, 43, 0.06)`.
3. **Level 2 (Hover & Focus States / Dropdowns):** Subtle elevation gain using `0 8px 24px rgba(6, 24, 43, 0.12)`.
4. **Level 3 (Fixed WhatsApp Dock & Overlays):** `0 12px 32px rgba(6, 24, 43, 0.22)`. Floating widgets retain separation without disconnecting from the page plane.

## Shapes

The shape system expresses corporate sobriety through disciplined, medium-curvature radii that preserve a friendly yet professional profile.

### Corner Radius Standards
- **Small (8px):** Chips, data badges, checkboxes, text input fields, and compact alerts.
- **Medium (12px):** Primary conversion buttons, secondary action buttons, dropdown panels, and input containers.
- **Large (16px):** Service cards, conversion highlight banners, and modal dialog containers.
- **Pill Shape (`9999px`):** Permitted only when functional clarity demands it (e.g., small status badges or discrete floating triggers). General CTAs strictly utilize the medium radius.

## Components

### Buttons & Conversion Actions
The interface enforces a strict two-tier conversion model without competing paths.
- **Primary CTA ("Agendar primera asesoría gratis"):**
  - Surface: `#E4B25F` (Gold)
  - Text: `#06182B` (Navy), 600 weight, 15px
  - Height: Minimum 48px (meets accessible 48×48px tap targets)
  - Padding: 0 24px
  - Corner radius: `12px`
  - Hover: Background shifts to `#ECBD6A`; subtle focus ring `2px solid #ECBD6A` with offset.
- **Secondary CTA ("Hablar por WhatsApp"):**
  - Surface: Transparent or `#071A2E`
  - Border: 1.5px solid `#E4B25F` (on navy) or `#06182B` (on light surfaces)
  - Text: `#E4B25F` or `#FFFFFF` (on navy)
  - Height: 48px, Corner radius: `12px`
  - Mobile docking: On mobile screens, WhatsApp remains accessible as an unobtrusive persistent floating icon with label docking cleanly at the viewport base.

### Cards & Service Containers
- Cards are applied selectively and never repeated consecutively as pure aesthetic filler.
- **Standard Service Card:** White (`#FFFFFF`) or Cream (`#F5F0E6`) surface, 1px border `rgba(6, 24, 43, 0.08)`, `16px` border radius, padding `32px`. Includes a linear gold icon, Montserrat H3 headline, descriptive text in `#111111`, and a secondary text CTA link.
- **Featured Service Card (Forensic Audit / Revisoría):** Navy `#071A2E` background, 1.5px gold border `#E4B25F`, white body text, and gold accent tags.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Base height: 52px with 16px horizontal internal padding.
- Radii: `8px` or `12px`.
- In dark containers: Surface `#071A2E`, border 1px solid `rgba(228, 178, 95, 0.3)`, text `#FFFFFF`, label floating or stacked with high contrast.
- Focus state: Outline `2px solid #E4B25F` with clear visual indicator.

### Trust Metrics & Accreditations
- Modular statistic components: Montserrat 700 40px numbers in Gold `#E4B25F`, paired with 14px Montserrat 500 labels in Cream `#F5F0E6` or muted Navy.
- Clean linear divider lines with 1px thickness and low-opacity gold accent.
